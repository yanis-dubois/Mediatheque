A local-first desktop application designed to centralize, manage, and explore your personal media collection (Books, Movies, Series, Video Games and Tabletop Games) where you own your data. Powered by **Tauri v2**, **Angular**, and **Rust**.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📦 Unified Media Management
Centralize and catalog your entire entertainment world under a single, cohesive dashboard:
*   **Multi-Category Support:** Full management for **Books**, **Movies**, **TV Series**, **Video Games**, and **Tabletop Games**.
*   **Metadata Enrichment:** Instantly search and import comprehensive details, covers, and structural data into your personal library.
*   **Custom Organization:** Group your media into tailored collections to stay organized.
*   **Progress Tracking:** Track movies you have watched, mark what you plan to read, or log TV series your currently watching.

### 🏠 Local-First & Privacy-Focused
Designed from the ground up to give you absolute control over your digital footprint:
*   **Offline Availability:** As a local-first application, all library data is stored natively on your machine. You can explore, sort, and organize your collection anytime, completely offline.
*   **100% Private:** There is no telemetry, no tracking, and absolutely no data collection on my end. Your data belongs to you.

### 🌐 Powered by the Community
This application relies on free, open, and community-driven databases to fetch media information.

*   **Providers:** Data came from HARDCOVER (https://hardcover.app) for books, TMDB (https://www.themoviedb.org) for movies and TV series, IGDB (https://www.igdb.com) for video games and BGG (https://boardgamegeek.com) for tabletop games.
*   **Crowdsourced Data:** If you encounter missing items, incorrect summaries, or incomplete metadata, you are highly encouraged to contribute directly to the respective upstream data providers to help improve the open ecosystem for everyone.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

-   **Frontend:** Angular (v18+), TypeScript, SCSS.
-   **Backend / Native Core:** Rust, Tauri v2.
